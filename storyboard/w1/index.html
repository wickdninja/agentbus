Watch opens on what needs you

Today watch shows everything at once, and a question for you looks like every other line. With W1 it starts with the few things only you can settle, then who is working, then everything else.

What counts as needing you

Two kinds of message, and only when they are sent to you by name.

A questionNeeds you until you answer it.
A handoffNeeds you until you reply to it, with anything.
OrderThe one that has waited longest comes first.
Working nowEvery agent holding files right now, with what it is doing. This takes the place of the Agents and Claims lists; both are one Tab away.

Turn it on

Watch needs to know which name is yours. Put this in your shell profile once:

export AGENTBUS_OWNER=nate

Your agents already send to you by this name. Without it, watch looks as it does today, with one line at the top saying how to turn this on.

How we will know it works

Two numbers, read from the bus on both machines, before and after W1 ships.

Time to your answerThe middle value, in minutes, from a question sent to you until your answer. Lower is better.
Left waitingHow many questions and handoffs to you waited more than an hour without a reply, per day. Lower is better.
