Demo: the tour plays inside watch

Today the tour finishes first and then offers watch, so a newcomer sees a bus where everything already happened. In PR B watch opens at once and the tour runs behind it, one step every two seconds, with the step and its command on the two rows above the keys.

D1Start

Two lines, then watch takes the screen. No question to answer first.

D2The whole tour, at its real pace

Every screen here is today's watch, captured while the demo's own steps ran against its bus; the caption rows, the header's "demo bus" and the colours are PR B. The last step is new: the lead takes the folder for review, so the claim changes hands and the mark shows.

D3Every step, at 60 columns

The same tour in a narrow terminal. The caption keeps its two rows; Messages gives way first.

D4After q

Quitting watch ends the tour, removes the bus and prints today's closing lines.

D5Not a terminal

Piped or in CI there is no watch and no pause: the tour prints as text and ends. Today it still pauses two seconds per step unless you pass --no-pause; PR B drops the pauses when stdout is not a terminal. This is today's text.
